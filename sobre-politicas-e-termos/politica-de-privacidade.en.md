# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 21, 2026

> This English version is provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the **Portuguese (Brazil) version prevails**.

Your privacy is a design principle of **Veredas Reader**. This Policy explains clearly which data is processed, by whom, for what purpose and how you can exercise your rights, in accordance with Brazil's **General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 – LGPD)** and the **Brazilian Civil Rights Framework for the Internet (Law No. 12,965/2014)**.

## 1. Who the controller is

The App is developed and made available by **Torres Software**, the brand used by independent developer **Maykow Roberto Lima Torres**, based in Pernambuco, Brazil, who is the controller of any personal data processed through the App, under article 5, VI, of the LGPD.

Data subject contact channel: **torres.software.app@gmail.com**

## 2. In a few lines

- There is no sign-up, login or account. **We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or ID.**
- Your books, notes, progress and shelf stay **only on your device**. Torres Software **does not receive** this data.
- Reading works **without internet**.
- In the **free version**, Google AdMob shows ads and collects some technical device data, as described in section 5.
- For the **subscription**, payment is handled by the app store. We never see your card details.

## 3. What we do NOT collect (privacy by design)

The App was built to work locally. Torres Software **does not collect, transmit or store on servers**:

- the files of your books, documents, comics, audiobooks or videos;
- your reading progress, time spent in the App, bookmarks, highlights or quotes;
- your notes, comments, collections, tags or shelf organization;
- your reading preferences (theme, font, margins, language and the like).

All of this is saved in the App's local storage, inside your device, and only you have access to it.

## 4. File access and permissions

4.1. The App only accesses the files and folders **you choose**, at the moment you choose them. The on-device book search only examines the folder you point to, and only when you ask. There is no automatic or background scanning.

4.2. **Read aloud.** This feature uses your device's operating system speech engine. Depending on the manufacturer and the voice selected, some speech engines may process the text online, under the system provider's policies. If you prefer, choose a voice installed on the device.

## 5. Ads in the free version (Google AdMob)

5.1. To keep the App free, the free version shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**, a Google service. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may automatically collect:

- the **device advertising identifier** (Advertising ID on Android, or IDFA on iOS when authorized). It is a code generated by the system that you can reset or disable, and it does not reveal your name or e-mail;
- **ad interaction data**, such as impressions and taps;
- **technical device and network data**: IP address (used for approximate location and security), connection type, manufacturer, model, language and operating system version.

5.2. **Legal bases (article 7 of the LGPD).** **Personalized** ads, based on interests, depend on your **consent**, which you may give or refuse and withdraw at any time. **Non-personalized** ads, based only on context and approximate location, rely on the **legitimate interest** in keeping the App free, while respecting your rights and expectations. Where the laws of your country require consent for any ad-related processing, consent will be requested.

5.3. Google acts as an independent controller of the data it collects through AdMob and processes it under its own privacy policy: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Learn how Google uses data from partner apps at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

5.4. **How to control ads.** You can:

- reset or delete the advertising identifier in your system settings (on Android, under **Settings > Google > Ads** or **Settings > Privacy > Ads**, depending on the device; on iOS, under **Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking**);
- manage personalization at [myadcenter.google.com](https://myadcenter.google.com);
- subscribe to the ad-free version, which shows no ads.

## 6. Subscription and payments

Subscribing, paying, renewing and cancelling are handled **entirely by the app store** (for example, Google Play or the App Store). Torres Software **does not collect, access or store** card numbers, tax IDs or bank details. The App only receives from the store a confirmation that the subscription is active, used solely to turn off ads.

## 7. Crash diagnostics

App stores may automatically collect aggregated crash and performance reports (for example, device model, system version and where in the code the failure occurred), according to your device settings and the store's policies. These reports do not include the content of your books or notes and are used only to fix bugs.

## 8. Data sharing

Torres Software **does not sell, rent or share** personal data. The only data flows with third parties are those described in sections 5, 6 and 7 (Google AdMob, the app store and the store's crash diagnostics). Data may also be provided to authorities when required by law or court order.

## 9. International transfers

Google and the app stores may process data on servers outside Brazil. These transfers take place under the cases provided in article 33 of the LGPD and with the safeguards adopted by those providers.

## 10. Backup

10.1. The backup file is created **on your device** and handed to you. **It is not sent to any Torres Software server.**

10.2. The backup contains your progress, marks, notes, shelf organization and cover thumbnails. **It does not contain the books.** The file is not encrypted: keep it as a personal document. When you send it by e-mail, cloud storage or another app, it also becomes subject to the privacy rules of the service you chose.

## 11. Security

Since your reading data stays on the device itself, its main protection is your device's. We recommend keeping the screen lock enabled and the system up to date. We take technical measures so that the App only accesses what you authorize and does not send your reading data off the device.

## 12. Retention and deletion

12.1. **Reading data:** stays on your device for as long as you want. You can delete books and notes in the App itself, and full removal happens when you **clear the App's data** or **uninstall it**.

12.2. **AdMob data:** kept by Google for the periods set out in Google's privacy policy.

## 13. Your rights (article 18 of the LGPD)

At any time you may request: confirmation that processing takes place; access to the data; correction of incomplete or outdated data; anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data; portability; information about with whom data is shared; and withdrawal of consent. You may also object to processing based on legitimate interest.

In practice:

- **Reading data:** since it exists only on your device, you access, correct and delete it yourself in the App. **Portability** is provided by the **Backup and restore** feature, which exports your data to a file.
- **Advertising data:** use the controls in section 5.4 and Google's tools.
- **Any other request:** write to **torres.software.app@gmail.com**. We will reply within the time limits set by the LGPD.

You also have the right to file a complaint with Brazil's **National Data Protection Authority (ANPD)**: [www.gov.br/anpd](https://www.gov.br/anpd). If you live outside Brazil, you may also contact the data protection authority of your country.

## 14. Children and teenagers

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Use by children and teenagers should take place with the knowledge and supervision of their parents or guardians, as provided in article 14 of the LGPD.

## 15. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the App or in the law. The date at the top shows the current version. Material changes, especially those involving new data processing, will be announced in the App before they take effect and, where necessary, your consent will be requested.
