# Veredas Reader

> **An e-book, comic and audiobook reader that keeps your library on your device.**

**Veredas Reader** opens **EPUB, MOBI, PDF, TXT, MD and DOCX** files, reads comics in **CBZ, CBR, CB7 and CBT**, plays audiobooks in **MP3 and M4B**, plays videos in **MP4**, keeps your shelf available without internet and preserves your highlights, quotes, notes and reading progress.

## What it does

- **Import and read without internet:** imports books from your device and keeps a local copy for reading, even in airplane mode.

- **Shelf organization:** organize by status, collections, series, authors and tags.

- **Notes and highlights:** highlight passages and create quotes, notes and bookmarks.

- **Reading customization:** adjust theme, typography, spacing, margins, brightness and page-turn style.

- **Comics:** pinch and double-tap zoom, two-page spreads in landscape, manga direction (right to left) and continuous webtoon-style scrolling. Title, series, author and cover are read from the file itself when it includes them.

- **Read aloud:** reads the text using the voices available on your device.

- **Audiobooks and videos:** chapters, bookmarks, adjustable speed, sleep timer and exact resume where you left off.

- **On-device conversion:** converts PDF to EPUB on the device itself, without sending the file anywhere.

- **Backup:** creates a file with your progress, marks and shelf, for you to keep wherever you like.

- **Languages:** the interface is available in several languages and follows your device's language.

## Technology

Reading, shelf organization and all your notes work **without internet**. Every component the reader needs (icons, PDF reading, DOCX reading, file and comic decompression) comes with the app and stays on your device.

Features that require a connection, such as showing ads in the free version, are described in the **Privacy Policy**. Open-source component licenses are available under **About > Open-source licenses**.

## Privacy

Your books, your shelf and your notes stay on your device and remain yours.
